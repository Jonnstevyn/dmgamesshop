DOS is the exciting sequel to UNO with a fresh gameplay mechanic. Players match numbers to a shared Center Row — using one card or two that add up to the value. Wild cards, colour bonuses, and the rule that you must shout DOS with two cards remaining makes every round fast and unpredictable.

How to Play

DOS Card Game is available now at DM Games Shop — order online or visit us at Simara Mall, 3rd Floor, Shop 42, Tom Mboya Street (Opposite Archives), Nairobi CBD. Here is a quick guide to get you started.

  1. Each player is dealt 7 cards. Two face-up cards form the starting Center Row.
  2. On your turn, match one or two cards to the numbers in the Center Row.
  3. A colour match using two cards earns a bonus — other players must draw extra cards.
  4. Wild DOS cards can substitute any number in the Center Row.
  5. Add a new card to the Center Row at the end of each turn.
  6. When you have 2 cards remaining, shout DOS or draw a penalty. First to empty hand wins.
