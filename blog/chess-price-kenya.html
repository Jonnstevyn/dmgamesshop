Which Type Should You Choose?

Magnetic sets

Magnetic sets keep their pieces in place, so they suit travel, younger players and anyone who plays on the move. Options include Brain Chess small, Brain Chess Large magnetic, Magnetic battle chess and the small 2-in-1 magnetic chess and checkers set.

2-in-1 and multi-game boards

If you want more than chess, the 2-in-1 sets add checkers, the 3-in-1 wooden chessboard adds a third game, and the 5-in-1 and 6-in-1 sets are good value for families who like variety.

Chess mats

A chess mat is a roll-up playing surface for clubs and schools. We have a 30cm squared mat (KSH 1,600) and large mats with extra queens (KSH 2,400).

Is Chess a Good Gift?

Chess suits most ages from about 6 and lasts for years. A compact magnetic set at KSH 999 is an easy gift under KSH 1,000.
