How to Play

Monopoly Board Game is available now at DM Games Shop — order online or visit us at Simara Mall, 3rd Floor, Shop No.42, Nairobi CBD. Here is a quick guide to get you started.

  1. Roll two dice and move your token clockwise around the board.
  2. Land on an unowned property — buy it from the bank at face value.
  3. Land on another player's property — pay them rent, more if they have houses or hotels.
  4. Own all properties in a colour group — build houses and hotels to maximise rent.
  5. Land on Go To Jail — move directly there, do not collect KSH 200.
  6. The last player remaining when all others are bankrupt wins.
