How to Play

A quick guide for new players.

  1. Each player holds a hand of cards from the deck.
  2. On your turn, play a card and place a chip on a matching space on the board.
  3. Then draw a replacement card.
  4. Two-eyed Jacks are wild. One-eyed Jacks remove an opponent's chip.
  5. A sequence is five chips in a row in any direction. The corner spaces count as free spaces.
  6. Two players or two teams need two sequences to win. With three teams, one sequence wins.
