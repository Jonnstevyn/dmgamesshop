How to Play

A quick guide for new players.

  1. Each player controls 16 pieces: a King, a Queen, 2 Rooks, 2 Bishops, 2 Knights and 8 Pawns.
  2. White moves first, then players alternate one move per turn.
  3. Each piece moves differently: Rooks in straight lines, Bishops diagonally, Knights in an L-shape, and the Queen in any direction.
  4. If your King is under attack (in check) you must move out of danger on your next turn.
  5. Checkmate, where the King is in check with no escape, ends the game.
  6. Special moves include castling, en passant and pawn promotion.
